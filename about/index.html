About Randomly Pick

Randomly Pick is a browser-based toolkit for fast and simple random decisions. It includes Wheel of Names, Lucky Draw, Ladder Draw, Team Split, Coin Flip, and Dice Roll.

Operator and Publisher

Randomly Pick is operated by Juntae Ko. Service questions, business inquiries, and bug reports are handled through the direct contact channel at newgotae@gmail.com.

What We Focus On

  • Fast input workflows for names and options
  • Clear, reproducible draw results
  • Practical tools for giveaways, classrooms, team splits, balanced team drafts, and events
  • Simple export and history handling where available

How It Works

Most tool interactions are processed directly in your browser for speed and usability. Randomly Pick is designed as a lightweight web app without account sign-in requirements.

Core draws and inputs are handled locally in the browser. Some convenience features may use browser storage such as localStorage, but participant lists and draw state are not stored on our servers unless a specific third-party integration requires it.

Advertising, Cookies, and Consent

The site may use cookies, browser storage, analytics, and advertising tools such as Google AdSense, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity, and Userback. These providers may process data under their own policies.

For visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, consent choices may be requested before personalized advertising is shown. If consent is unavailable, advertising may be limited or non-personalized.
