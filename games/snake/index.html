How to read the game

Snake stays intentionally simple: one board, one score, one target. That keeps the game easy to test and easy to understand on both mobile and desktop.

Quick controls

Use the keyboard, tap the arrows, or swipe on the board. Press Space to pause, Enter to start, and R to reset.

How to play

Three short steps are enough to keep the game readable and the score loop obvious.

1. Start the round. Press Start or use any direction key to move off the ready state.
2. Eat the food. Each piece grows the snake and raises your score, so the board gets tighter over time.
3. Avoid walls and your body. Reach the target score for a clean win, or reset and try a better route.

When this game fits
