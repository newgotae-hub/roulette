Short guide

Connect Four works because the rules are easy to understand, but the position quickly becomes interesting. This page keeps the experience calm, quick, and native to Randomly Pick.

How to Play

1. Choose a mode. Play local two-player on one device or switch to CPU for a solo round.
2. Tap a column. The disc falls to the lowest open slot in that column.
3. Connect four. Horizontal, vertical, and diagonal lines all count.

When to Use It

Quick match Great when you want a short game between tasks.
Family or friends Easy to explain, easy to play, and no extra setup needed.
Mobile webview The controls are column-based, so the game feels natural on smaller screens.
