How to play

Snake keeps the rules simple, but now adds mode-specific pacing and a streak layer so each run has a reason to keep going. That keeps the game readable on mobile and desktop without feeling flat.

Quick controls

Keyboard, arrow buttons, and swipe are all supported. Press Space to pause, Enter to start, and R to reset.

FAQ

The best score stays in your browser only. If you keep eating without dropping the pace, your streak grows and makes bonus apples more valuable.

How to play

Keep it short: start, eat, avoid the walls, and use the streak layer to turn a clean run into a better score chase.

1. Start the round. Press Start or tap a direction to begin moving.
2. Build a streak. Consecutive clean apples keep the streak alive and turn the next bonus apple into a bigger payoff.
3. Avoid walls and your body. Clear the target score without crashing into the walls or yourself.

When this game fits

It works best when you want something familiar, quick, and low-friction.
