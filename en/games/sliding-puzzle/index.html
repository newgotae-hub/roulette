How to play

The rules stay simple, and the return path stays short.

1. Slide tiles into the blank

Tap or use the arrows. Only the tiles that touch the blank can move.

2. Restore the order

Put the numbers back in order and leave the blank in the bottom-right corner.

Mode note

Classic is free play, Timed adds pressure, and Daily uses today’s shared board.

When it fits

It works for a quick reset or a slower puzzle session.

Short focus reset

Even a couple of moves change the board immediately, which makes it good for a quick mental reset.

Repeat play

Timed and Daily both give you a reason to replay the same style of board and do better.
