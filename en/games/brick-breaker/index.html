How it works

Brick Breaker is about angle control. The rules stay simple so the game can feel quick to restart, easy to read, and useful as a compact arcade tab inside Randomly Pick.

Review points

The top of the board stays focused on bricks, the bottom stays focused on the paddle, and the in-between space makes the ball motion easy to track without extra noise.

Why it fits Randomly Pick

The goal is a practical, in-site arcade tab that feels like part of the product rather than a separate neon microsite.

How to play

Keep the ball alive, clear one side first, and shape the next shot by where the paddle sends the bounce.

1. Move the paddle. Left and right motion changes the bounce angle.
2. Break the bricks. Durable bricks take two hits, standard bricks take one.
3. Protect your lives. Miss the ball and you lose a life. Lose them all and the round ends.

When it fits

Brick Breaker works well when you want something faster than a puzzle but still easy to restart after one short run.

Short arcade breaks Each round is short enough for quick sessions and easy restarts.
Score chasing The score, combo, and best run are visible immediately, which makes replay natural.
Touch + keyboard demos The same control set feels natural on desktop and mobile without extra setup.
