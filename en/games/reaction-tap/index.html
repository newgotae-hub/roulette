How it works

Short rounds, a fast reset loop, and a touch-friendly target make it easy to replay on mobile or in a webview.

Modes

Classic

The shortest possible test. Tap once, get one reaction time, and restart in seconds.

Sprint

Five rounds in a row. It is better when you want a small rhythm and an average to beat.

Daily Challenge

Everyone gets the same seed for the day. It gives you a simple reason to come back and compare again.

Mode note

Classic is one clean tap, Sprint strings together five quick rounds, and Daily Challenge uses today’s shared seed.

How to play

Wait for the signal, tap once, and keep going. The game is built for quick restarts and short sessions.

1. Tap the big button to start. The first tap begins the wait for the signal.
2. Tap as soon as it turns green. Faster taps produce shorter reaction times.
3. Replay immediately. Sprint and Daily Challenge move to the next round fast so the loop stays snappy.

When it fits

It works well when you want a tiny focus reset, a quick score to beat, or a game that feels good in a mobile webview.

Short breaks One round is over almost instantly, so it works during coffee breaks or between tasks.
Score chasing Fastest reaction and average reaction are visible right away, which makes personal improvement obvious.
Touch-first demos The whole game is driven by one large tap target, so it feels natural on phones and embedded browsers.
