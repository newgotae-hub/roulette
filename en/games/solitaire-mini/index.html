How it works

The goal is a short, clear solitaire loop that is easy to restart.

Modes

Classic is free practice, Timed gives you one minute, and Daily uses a shared seed so everyone works from the same setup.

Replay loop

Draw card, Auto move, and Undo stay visible so the game feels fast and readable on repeat visits.

When it fits

It is a good fit when you want something calmer than Snake, but still want a real game with a board you can read at a glance.

How to play

Keep the rules simple and the board readable.

1. Draw a card. Tap Stock to move one card into Waste.
2. Build foundations by suit. A through K in order, one suit per pile.
3. Open lanes in tableau. Move cards around to expose the next playable cards.

When this game fits

Best for thoughtful short breaks and repeat runs.

Short breaks One run is enough, and the board is easy to restart when you only have a minute.
Daily challenge The daily seed gives the game a small return loop without needing accounts or leaderboards.
Phone and webview Large tap targets and compact card stacks make the game comfortable on smaller screens.
