About Randomly Pick

Randomly Pick is a browser-based toolkit for fast and simple random decisions. It includes Wheel of Names, Lucky Draw, Ladder Draw, Team Split, Coin Flip, and Dice Roll.

Operator and Publisher

Randomly Pick is operated by Juntae Ko. Service questions, business inquiries, and bug reports are handled through newgotae@gmail.com.

Why the site includes guides as well as tools

Random tools alone do not explain how to run a trustworthy draw. In real events, classrooms, streams, and group activities, users need to understand who is included, which rules apply, and how results can be reviewed later. That is why Randomly Pick publishes operation guides and policy pages alongside the interactive tools.

Content and guide standards

The guide pages are original site content written around actual tool usage and operating decisions. The focus is not on filling space with keywords, but on explaining practical rules such as list checks, exclusion handling, tool selection, classroom usage, and winner-record management.

The English guide hub covers tool comparison, fair draw operation, event checklists, classroom usage, and balanced team generation.

What We Focus On

  • Fast input workflows for names and options
  • Clear, reproducible draw results and records
  • Practical tools for events, classrooms, team splits, and balanced drafts
  • Simple export and history handling where available

How It Works

Most tool interactions are processed directly in your browser for speed and usability. The site is designed as a lightweight web app without account sign-in requirements.

Core inputs and draw states are handled locally in the browser. Some convenience features may use localStorage, but participant lists and draw state are not stored on our servers unless a specific third-party integration requires it.

Validation before deployment

Before deployment, the site checks metadata, policy links, sitemap coverage, guide discoverability, and the content blocks used for ad-review readiness. The review is not limited to whether the tool works; it also checks whether the surrounding explanation, trust links, and discovery paths remain intact.

Advertising, Cookies, and Consent

The site may use Google AdSense, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity, Userback, and similar tools. These providers may process data under their own policies.

For visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, consent choices may be requested before personalized advertising is shown. If consent is unavailable, advertising may be limited or non-personalized.
